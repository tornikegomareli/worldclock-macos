Earth imagery: NASA Earth Observatory (Blue Marble, Black Marble).

Stylized terrain: Jesse Allen, NASA Earth Observatory, using GEBCO data
produced by the British Oceanographic Data Centre.

earth-elevation.jpg is the unmodified 5400 × 2700 Blue Marble elevation
map, with grayscale values representing 0–6400 metres. The app uses it
for exaggerated shaded relief, not surveyed terrain or navigation.
